## About

I work across the stack, from backend systems and data models to responsive interfaces and refined interactions. I care about the intersection of software engineering and product design: not just making things work, but making them work well.

I hold a degree in Computer Systems Engineering from Universidad Latina de Costa Rica, and I am continuing my studies in software engineering at UIA while also pursuing a degree in Education at UAM.

At UMP I build digital solutions for the company and its clients. Through **Sorin Labs**, my independent studio, I design and ship full stack applications, business websites, internal tools and custom APIs.

> Good software is intentional. The goal is not to use the most technology, it is to build the right thing, well.
