## About

I'm a software engineer from Costa Rica with a background in Computer Systems Engineering. Most of my work involves web development, building applications, working with databases and figuring out how to turn ideas into something people can actually use.

I currently work on digital solutions at Ultimate Media Productions and develop independent projects through Sorin Labs, my own development studio.

Outside of client work, I spend time building things that interest me, experimenting with technologies and working on projects connected to my hometown, Limón.
